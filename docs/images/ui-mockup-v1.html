You

We sell an adaptive test that places new students at our partner language schools into levels A1 to C1. Do the high-risk rules already apply to us?

Eliza

Level placement is one of the education use cases in Annex III. For these systems, the consolidated text of 27 July 2026 gives 2 December 2027 as the date, not 2 August 2026.

Conditions and exceptions

Annex III, point 3(c) lists AI systems intended to be used for assessing the appropriate level of education that an individual will receive or will be able to access, in educational and vocational training institutions.1 Systems listed in Annex III are considered high-risk.2 Article 6(3) sets out when such a system is not considered high-risk, for example when it only performs a narrow procedural task, and a system that profiles natural persons always stays high-risk.3

Article 113 gives 2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk under Article 6(2) and Annex III.4 The Regulation in general applies from 2 August 2026.5

Whether your test falls under these provisions is a decision for your legal team. The provisions above are the ones it depends on.
